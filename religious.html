Religious Places

Discover the spiritual diversity and rich religious heritage of Hawassa

St. Gabriel Monastery

St. Gabriel Monastery, located in the heart of Hawassa, is one of the most important religious landmarks in Southern Ethiopia. The church is known for its beautiful architecture, including a shining golden dome and colorful Orthodox murals that decorate the interior.

It follows the traditions of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church and functions both as a church and a monastic center. Every year, thousands of pilgrims visit during the Feast of Archangel Gabriel, celebrated on July 27 and December 27.

Holy Trinity Cathedral

Hawassa Holy Trinity Cathedral is one of the major Ethiopian Orthodox centers in the city. Known for its beautiful architecture, church school, and peaceful environment, the Cathedral also supports elderly people who live within its compound.

It serves as a spiritual, educational, and community center where believers gather for worship, holidays, and cultural ceremonies.

Medhanialem Church

Medhanialem Ethiopian Orthodox Church in Hawassa is a deeply historical and spiritual site rooted in centuries of faith. The church houses valuable relics, including ancient parchments, decorated ecclesiastical clothing, and historic furniture.

A notable highlight is its traditional bell, imported from Poland during the reign of Emperor Tewodros II, and the "Stone of Tewodros," said to be used by the emperor himself. Its serene atmosphere makes it a must-visit destination in Hawassa.

Kidane Mehret Catholic Cathedral

Kidane Mehret Cathedral is the main Catholic cathedral in Hawassa and serves as the spiritual center of the Apostolic Vicariate of Awasa. It is one of the city's most important Christian landmarks, known for its peaceful atmosphere and welcoming community.

The cathedral hosts regular Mass services, religious celebrations, youth programs, and community support activities. It continues to play an important role in spiritual life, education, and community development in Hawassa.

Central Mosque (Arab Sefer)

In Hawassa's Zuria district, there is a prominent mosque often referenced as the Arab Sefer Mosque. This place of worship serves a vibrant Muslim community in the city and stands as a symbol of religious diversity in Hawassa.

The mosque is part of Hawassa's rich cultural landscape, coexisting with Christian communities and reflecting the city's multi-faith heritage. For those interested in religious tourism, visiting this mosque provides insight into how Islam fits into Hawassa's broader spiritual fabric.

Kale Heywet Church

Hawassa Tabor Kale Heywet Church is a prominent Protestant congregation located in the Zuria district of Hawassa. As part of the evangelical Kale Heywet denomination, this church plays a vital role in the spiritual and social life of local Christians.

Known for its welcoming fellowship, the church hosts weekly worship services, outreach programs, and community events. Visiting here provides a window into modern Protestant life in Hawassa.

Full Gospel Believers' Church

Hawassa Full Gospel Believers' Church, also known as Mulu Wongel (ሙሉ ወንጌል), is a vibrant Pentecostal congregation in Hawassa. Known for its energetic worship services, powerful preaching, and dynamic choirs, the church plays a major role in the spiritual life of many in the city.

It maintains an active online presence, with regular broadcasts on YouTube and community engagement through Telegram. Visiting this church offers a window into the modern, Spirit-filled aspect of Christianity in Hawassa.
