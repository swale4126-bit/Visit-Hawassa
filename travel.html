Travel Tips

Safety in Hawassa

Hawassa is known for being a generally safe and welcoming city, offering a peaceful environment for visitors and residents. Most areas around the lake, city center, and main roads are comfortable to explore during the day,Transportation options like bajaj and taxis are widely available and safe to use, provided fares are agreed on beforehand. While enjoying the lakeside, visitors should avoid approaching wildlife such as hippos and refrain from swimming in unknown areas. Overall, Hawassa provides a pleasant and secure experience as long as basic safety precautions are followed.

Health Tips

Hawassa is known for its clean and refreshing environment, thanks to its lakeside location and well-maintained public areas. The city has a reputation for being one of the cleaner and more organized cities in southern Ethiopia, with regular street cleaning and a strong focus on keeping tourist areas tidy.Visitors are encouraged to help maintain Hawassa’s natural beauty and cleanliness by disposing of trash properly and keeping public spaces tidy.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Hawassa is during the dry seasons, from October to February and June to early August, when the weather is sunny, warm, and perfect for exploring the city and enjoying outdoor activities. During these months, visitors can take boat rides on Lake Hawassa, walk along the lakeside, and enjoy parks and markets comfortably. Traveling during this period also allows tourists to experience local cultural and religious events, such as Fichee-Chambalaalla, the Sidama New Year festival in June–July, and Saint Gabriel celebrations in July and December, which feature traditional ceremonies, prayers, and vibrant community gatherings. Avoiding the heavy rainy months of April and May ensures a smoother, more enjoyable trip with plenty of opportunities for sightseeing and cultural experiences
