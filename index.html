Top Attractions

Explore the most popular destinations in our beautiful city

Lake Hawassa

A beautiful freshwater lake in the Great Rift Valley, famous for its hippos and birdlife. Enjoy a boat ride or simply relax by the shore while watching the sunset.

Amora Gedel Park

A perfect spot for bird watching and relaxation. Get close to nature and see Marabou storks, monkeys, and other wildlife in their natural habitat.

Fish Market

Experience the local culture and fresh fish at the famous "Amora Gedel" fish market. Taste the delicious fish soup and grilled tilapia fresh from the lake.

Tabor Mountain

Hiking trails with panoramic views of the city and the lake. A moderate hike that rewards you with breathtaking scenery, especially at sunrise or sunset.
