Resorts & Hotels

Haile Resort Hawassa

One of Hawassa’s premier lakeside resorts, offering stunning views of Lake Hawassa. Features include a luxurious spa, outdoor swimming pool, boat trips, and fine dining. Perfect for a relaxing getaway.

Lewi Resort and Spa

Located directly on the lakefront, Lewi Resort is known for its natural scenery and friendly service. Enjoy the spa, gym, and sauna, or take a boat ride to see the hippos.

Kerawud International Hotel

A highly-rated 4-star hotel offering excellent amenities including a 24-hour front desk and comfortable accommodations. A great choice for both business and leisure travelers.

Central Hawassa Hotel

Known for its modern design and well-proportioned rooms. Guests appreciate the attentive staff, swimming pool, and convenient location in the heart of the city.
