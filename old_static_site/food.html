Food & Holidays

Taste of Hawassa

From fresh fish straight from the lake to authentic Ethiopian spices and international cuisine, Hawassa offers a diverse culinary experience.

Traditional Ethiopian

For an authentic experience, visit Fenet Gurage Kettefo or Gored Gored Restaurant. Don't miss the chance to try Kitfo and Gored Gored, served with warm hospitality.

Fresh Seafood

The Fish Market (Amora Gedel) is legendary. Try the fresh fish soup and grilled tilapia. For a sit-down meal, Dabash Fish House offers delightful seafood dishes with local flavors.

International & Italian

Craving pizza or pasta? Dolce Vita and Venezia serve authentic Italian cuisine. For a mix of local and international dishes in a lively atmosphere, check out Gebre Restaurant.

Must-Try Delicacies

Fetto Fetto

Raw fish delicacy, a local favorite.

Chechebsa

Fried flatbread with spices and yogurt.

Fish Soup

Spicy and flavorful, perfect for breakfast.
